    \caption{Fund Performance Analysis: Returns and Alphas}\label{tab:fund_performance}
    \footnotesize{
    This table reports the analysis of fund performance around livestreaming events. Panel A reports the univariate mean differences in performance between livestreaming funds and their same-family non-livestreaming benchmarks ($Performance_{Livestream} - Performance_{Non-Livestream}$). Panels B and C report Generalized Difference-in-Differences (DiD) estimates from the following fixed-effects model:
    \[ Y_{i,m+k} = \beta_k \cdot \text{Event}_{i,m} + \gamma' \text{Controls}_{i,q-1} + \mu_i + \delta_m + \varepsilon_{i,m} \]
    where $Y_{i,m+k}$ is the fund performance (Return, FF3 Alpha, or FF5 Alpha) in the $k$-th month relative to the event month $m$. For Panel B (Own Effect), $\text{Event}_{i,m}$ is a dummy variable equal to one if fund $i$ is featured in at least one livestream in month $m$. For Panel C (Sibling Spillover), $\text{Event}_{i,m}$ is a dummy variable equal to one if fund $i$'s family conducts at least one livestream in month $m$, but fund $i$ is not featured in any of them. $\text{Controls}_{i,q-1}$ represents control variables from the most recent quarter, including Past Return, Top 5 Return, Size, Large, Age, Old, Management Fee, Service Fee, and Loads. All models include fund ($\mu_i$) and month ($\delta_m$) fixed effects. Robust $t$-statistics clustered at the family level are reported in parentheses. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively.}
