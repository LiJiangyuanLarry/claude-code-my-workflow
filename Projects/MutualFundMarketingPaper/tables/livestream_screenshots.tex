\section{Appendix: Visual Documentation of the Livestreaming Interface}\label{app:app:visual_interface_docs}

This appendix provides a structured visual walkthrough of the livestreaming ecosystem on the platform, documenting the user journey from initial discovery to active engagement and financial transaction. 
Figure~\ref{fig:app_portal} illustrates the \textit{Platform Portal Interface}, highlighting the search and discovery modules. 
Figure~\ref{fig:app_interface} details the \textit{In-stream Livestreaming Interface}, which captures the real-time interactive components during a live session. 
Finally, Figure~\ref{fig:app_gallery} demonstrates the \textit{Product Gallery}, representing the integrated subscription gateway for investment products.

            \begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\arabic*)}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]

                \item \textbf{Risk Disclosure:} Regulatory notices featuring fund risk warnings and suitability disclaimers.
                \item \textbf{Product Basket:} Entry point showing featured products, fund details, and purchase options.
                \item \textbf{Engagement Tools:} Interface elements for sharing the session and allowing viewers to express support through real-time likes.
