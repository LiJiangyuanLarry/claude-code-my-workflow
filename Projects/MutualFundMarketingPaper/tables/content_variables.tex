\subsection{Correlations among Livestream Content Characteristics}\label{subsec:correlations}

Table~\ref{correlations} presents correlations among 16 livestream content characteristics extracted from 795 mutual fund livestream transcripts. The lower triangle reports Pearson correlations; the upper triangle reports Spearman rank correlations.
The correlation structure reveals three distinct content strategy dimensions. Sales-oriented tactics exhibit strong co-occurrence: Sales Pressure, Promotion Density, and Urgency/Scarcity correlate between 0.67 and 0.71, while Return-Chasing Promotion correlates 0.57 with Past Performance Focus. This clustering suggests fund managers deploy aggressive sales tactics systematically rather than in isolation.

Educational content characteristics form a second cluster. Education Density correlates 0.37--0.46 with Jargon Complexity and positively with Risk Transparency (0.28--0.45) and Authority Credibility (0.30--0.43). Critically, educational dimensions correlate negatively with sales tactics: Education Density exhibits correlations of -0.28 to -0.35 with Sales Pressure, Promotion Density, and Urgency/Scarcity. This negative relationship suggests a fundamental trade-off between informative and persuasive content strategies.

Community engagement metrics constitute a third dimension. Interaction Responsiveness and Community Feeling correlate 0.64--0.67, the strongest relationship in the matrix. Social Proof Reliance correlates moderately with both Community Feeling (0.44--0.45) and emotional content dimensions, indicating a distinct engagement strategy centered on fostering viewer connection.
Language style variables reveal additional strategic choices. Language Informality correlates negatively with Jargon Complexity (-0.31 to -0.35) and Authority Credibility (-0.33 to -0.36), while Emotional Intensity and Storytelling Intensity correlate 0.46--0.51, suggesting presenters choose between technical credibility and emotional accessibility.
