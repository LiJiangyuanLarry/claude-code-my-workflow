        \footnotesize
        This table reports regression results for the effect of livestreaming on family return. The estimations are based on the following two-way fixed effects (TWFE) model:
        \begin{equation*}
            Y_{j,q} = \alpha_j + \delta_q + \beta_1 \text{Livestream}_{j,q} + \mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1} \Gamma + \epsilon_{j,q}
        \end{equation*}
        where $Y_{j,q}$ is the Family Return for family $j$ in quarter $q$. In Columns (1)-(3), the dependent variable is measured contemporaneously ($Y_{j,q}$), while in Columns (4)-(6), it is the one-quarter-ahead lead ($Y_{j,q+1}$). The key variable, $\text{Treatment}_{j,q}$, represents a measure of livestreaming activity. $\mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1}$ is a vector of control variables from the prior quarter, and $\alpha_j$ and $\delta_q$ are family and quarter fixed effects, respectively. Robust $t$-statistics are reported in parentheses. Standard errors are clustered at the family level. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively. See Appendix~\ref{tab:vardefs} for detailed variable definitions. % chktex 36
