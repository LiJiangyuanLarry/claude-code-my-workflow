      \caption{Fund Fee Analysis: Management Fee, Service Fee, and Loads}\label{tab:fund_fee}
      \footnotesize{
      This table reports the analysis of fund fee components around livestreaming events. Panel A reports the univariate
  mean differences in fee components between livestreaming funds and their same-family non-livestreaming benchmarks
  ($Fee_{Livestream} - Fee_{Non\mbox{-}Livestream}$). Panels B and C report Generalized Difference-in-Differences (DiD)
  estimates from the following fixed-effects model:
      \[
      Y_{i,q+k} = \beta_k \cdot \text{Event}_{i,q} + \gamma' \text{Controls}_{i,q-1} + \mu_i + \delta_q +
  \varepsilon_{i,q}
      \]
      where $Y_{i,q+k}$ is the fund fee component (Management Fee, Service Fee, or Loads) in the $k$-th quarter relative
  to event quarter $q$. For Panel B (Own Effect), $\text{Event}_{i,q}$ is a dummy variable equal to one if fund $i$ is
  featured in at least one livestream in quarter $q$. For Panel C (Sibling Spillover), $\text{Event}_{i,q}$ is a dummy
  variable equal to one if fund $i$'s family conducts at least one livestream in quarter $q$, but fund $i$ is not
  featured in any of them. $\text{Controls}_{i,q-1}$ represents lagged control variables from the previous quarter,
  including Past Return, Top 5 Return, Size, Large, Age, Old, Management Fee, Service Fee, and Loads. All models include
  fund ($\mu_i$) and quarter ($\delta_q$) fixed effects. Robust $t$-statistics clustered at the family level are
  reported in parentheses. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote
  statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively.}
