        This table reports regression results for the effect of livestreaming on family flow. The estimations are based on the following two-way fixed effects (TWFE) model:
        \begin{equation*}
            Y_{j,q} = \alpha_j + \delta_q + \beta_1 \text{Livestream}_{j,q} + \mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1} \Gamma + \epsilon_{j,q}
        \end{equation*}
        where $Y_{j,q}$ is the Family Flow for family $j$ in quarter $q$. Columns (1)--(3) use \textit{Family First Livestream}, \textit{Family Post Livestream}, and \textit{Family Livestream}, respectively. Column (4) uses \textit{Family Market Share}. Column (5) uses \textit{Market Livestream (Oth)}. Column (6) uses \textit{Family Livestream Count}. Columns (7) and (8) jointly include own-family and market-level livestream intensity measures. $\mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1}$ is a vector of control variables from the prior quarter, and $\alpha_j$ and $\delta_q$ are family and quarter fixed effects, respectively. Standard errors are clustered at the family level. Robust $t$-statistics are reported in parentheses. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively.
