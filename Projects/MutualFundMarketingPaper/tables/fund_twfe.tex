    \caption{Dynamic Livestream Effect on Fund Flow (TWFE)}\label{tab:fund_twfe}

    \begin{minipage}{0.95\textwidth}
        \footnotesize
        This table reports regression results for the effect of livestreaming on fund flow. The estimations are based on the following two-way fixed effects (TWFE) model:
        \begin{equation*}
            \text{Fund Flow}_{i,q} = \alpha_i + \delta_q + \beta_1  \text{Fund Livestream}_{i,q} + \beta_2 \text{Family Livestream (Oth)}_{i,q} + \mathbf{X}'_{i,q-1} \Gamma + \epsilon_{i,q}
        \end{equation*}
        where $Y_{i,j,q}$ is the Fund Flow for fund $i$ of family $j$ in quarter $q$. \textit{Fund Livestream} indicates whether fund $i$ was featured in any livestream during quarter $q$. \textit{Family Livestream (Oth)} indicates whether the fund's family livestreamed other funds (excluding fund $i$ itself) during quarter $q$. $\mathbf{X}'_{i,q-1}$ is a vector of control variables from the prior quarter, and $\alpha_i$ and $\delta_q$ are fund and quarter fixed effects, respectively. Robust $t$-statistics are reported in parentheses. Standard errors are clustered at the family level. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively. See Appendix~\ref{tab:vardefs} for detailed variable definitions.
    \end{minipage}
