        \footnotesize{
        This table reports regression results for the effect of livestreaming on family fee structures. The estimations are based on the following two-way fixed effects (TWFE) model:
        \[ Y_{j,q} = \alpha_j + \delta_q + \beta_1 \text{Livestream}_{j,q} + \mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1} \Gamma + \epsilon_{j,q} \]
        Columns 1--4 report results for Family Management Fee, Columns 5--8 for Family Service Fee, and Columns 9--12 for Family Loads. For each fee type, the odd-numbered pairs (e.g., Cols 1--2) examine the contemporaneous effect ($Y_{t}$), while the even-numbered pairs (e.g., Cols 3--4) examine the one-quarter-ahead lead effect ($Y_{t+1}$). The key variable, $\text{Treatment}_{j,q}$, represents a measure of livestreaming activity. $\mathbf{X}'_{j,q-1}$ is a vector of control variables from the prior quarter, and $\alpha_j$ and $\delta_q$ are family and quarter fixed effects, respectively. Robust $t$-statistics clustered at the family level are reported in parentheses. All continuous variables are winsorized at the 1\% and 99\% levels. *, **, and *** denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively. See Appendix B for detailed variable definitions.}
