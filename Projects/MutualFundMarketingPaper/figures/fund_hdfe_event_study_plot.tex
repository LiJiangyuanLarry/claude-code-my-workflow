        \textit{Note:} This figure plots the estimated dynamic response of fund flows to the first livestream conducted by their respective fund families. The estimates are the coefficients from the high-dimensional fixed effects (HDFE) difference-in-differences model at the fund level. The horizontal axis represents quarters relative to the first livestream event, with Quarter 0 being the event quarter and Quarter -1 serving as the baseline period. The solid blue dots represent the point estimates, and the vertical lines indicate the 95\% confidence intervals. For visualization, the endpoints of the event window are binned: the estimate at Quarter +4 aggregates all periods from Quarter +4 onwards, and the estimate at Quarter -4 aggregates all periods from Quarter -4 and earlier. See Appendix~\ref{tab:vardefs} for detailed variable definitions.
